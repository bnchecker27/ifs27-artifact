BayesL — iFS 2027 Artifact

This is the artifact accompanying the anonymous submission “BayesL: A Logic for the Verification of Bayesian Networks” to iFS 2027.

Interactive demonstrator

The demonstrator runs entirely in your browser (WebAssembly; a one-time download of about 3 MB). No installation, no server, no data leaves your machine.

Launch the demonstrator

Tested with current Chrome, Firefox and Safari.

Code and experiments

The full artifact — model checker source, benchmark models, experiment definitions and pre-computed results — is in this repository. Download it as a zip archive via the repository view, or see README.txt for reproduction instructions.

  • README.txt — getting started, smoke test, step-by-step reproduction of the paper’s tables
  • Dockerfile — one-command environment
  • run_experiments.lua — rerun all experiments
  • overleaf/experiments/ — experiment definitions and results as reported in the paper
